Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 21, 2026

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to CineGo. CineGo is a movie ticket booking platform designed to help users discover movies, explore theatres, view show timings, select seats, check offers and complete movie ticket bookings through a convenient digital experience.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information that you provide while using the CineGo website.

This Privacy Policy explains what information CineGo may collect, how that information may be used, how it may be protected and the choices available to you regarding your information.

By accessing or using CineGo, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CineGo, different types of information may be collected or processed.

2.1 Account Information

When you create or use a CineGo account, we may process information such as:

  • Your name or display name.
  • Your email address.
  • Login and authentication information.
  • Profile preferences.

2.2 Booking Information

When you make a movie booking, CineGo may process information required to create and manage the booking, including:

  • Selected movie.
  • Selected theatre.
  • Selected date and show time.
  • Selected screen.
  • Selected seats.
  • Number of tickets.
  • Booking reference information.
  • Transaction-related information.

2.3 Payment Information

If payment functionality is provided through a third-party payment service, payment information may be processed directly by that payment provider.

CineGo should not store complete card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs, banking passwords or other sensitive payment credentials unless a secure payment infrastructure specifically requires and supports such storage.

2.4 Location Information

CineGo may request access to your device or browser location to help identify nearby theatres and provide location-based recommendations.

Location access is controlled by your browser or device permissions. You can deny location access through your browser settings.

2.5 Device and Technical Information

When you access CineGo, certain technical information may automatically become available, including:

  • Browser type.
  • Device type.
  • Operating system.
  • IP address.
  • Screen resolution.
  • General usage information.
  • Diagnostic information.

3. How We Use Your Information

CineGo may use information collected through the website for purposes related to providing, maintaining and improving the service.

  • To create and manage your CineGo account.
  • To authenticate users.
  • To process movie ticket bookings.
  • To display movies, theatres and show timings.
  • To manage seat selection and booking details.
  • To provide booking confirmations.
  • To provide location-based theatre suggestions.
  • To remember selected preferences.
  • To provide offers and promotional features.
  • To improve website performance.
  • To improve user experience.
  • To prevent unauthorized activity.
  • To troubleshoot technical issues.

4. Cookies and Local Storage

CineGo may use browser technologies such as cookies, session storage and local storage to provide website functionality.

These technologies may be used to remember information such as login state, user preferences, selected filters, claimed offers and temporary website settings.

Examples of information stored locally

  • Login status.
  • User name or profile information.
  • User preferences.
  • Selected movie filters.
  • Claimed promotional offers.
  • Temporary booking-related information.

Clearing your browser cookies or local storage may remove certain CineGo preferences or locally stored information.

5. Movie and Theatre Information

CineGo displays information relating to movies, theatres, show timings, languages, formats, ratings, posters, trailers and other entertainment-related content.

Some movie or entertainment information may be obtained from external APIs, content providers or publicly available sources.

Movie availability, theatre information, show timings, ratings and other third-party information may change without prior notice.

6. Third-Party Services

CineGo may use third-party services to provide certain website functionality.

These services may include movie information APIs, video hosting platforms, payment providers, analytics services, authentication services, maps or location services and other technology providers.

Embedded Videos

CineGo may embed movie trailers or promotional videos from third-party video platforms such as YouTube.

When you interact with an embedded video, the third-party provider may collect information according to its own privacy policy.

CineGo does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

7. Payment and Transaction Security

CineGo may provide payment functionality through secure third-party payment providers.

Payment providers may process transaction information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

CineGo will take reasonable steps to avoid storing sensitive payment credentials such as card PINs, CVVs, passwords or banking authentication information.

8. Offers, Coupons and Promotions

CineGo may provide promotional offers, discount coupons, food and beverage offers, bank offers and other promotional benefits.

Information related to claiming or using an offer may be stored to prevent duplicate claims and to provide the requested promotional functionality.

Promotional offers may have individual terms, expiry dates, eligibility requirements and usage restrictions.

9. Communications

CineGo may use your contact information to provide important service-related communications.

  • Booking confirmations.
  • Account-related notifications.
  • Security notifications.
  • Customer support responses.

Promotional communications may be provided where permitted and where applicable preferences or consent have been obtained.

10. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information associated with CineGo against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, online service or electronic transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should avoid sharing passwords or authentication information with others.

11. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide CineGo services, maintain account functionality, complete transactions, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes and enforce applicable agreements.

Information stored only in your browser may remain available until you clear the relevant browser storage or reset the browser or device.

12. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information.

  • Request access to information associated with your account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Withdraw certain browser permissions.
  • Clear locally stored browser information.
  • Contact CineGo regarding privacy-related questions.

13. Children's Privacy

CineGo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent where such consent is required by applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to CineGo without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that the information can be reviewed and, where appropriate, removed.

14. Third-Party Websites and Links

CineGo may contain links to external websites, payment services, movie platforms, social media services or other third-party websites.

These websites operate independently from CineGo and may have their own privacy policies and terms of service.

CineGo is not responsible for the privacy practices, security or content of third-party websites.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

CineGo may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology, services, legal requirements or privacy practices.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page along with the revised "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how their information is handled.
